Privacy Policy for Order Ledger

本政策说明“订单台账”如何处理您在应用内创建的订单、采购、库存、BOM 和附件资料。

This policy explains how Order Ledger handles the orders, purchases, inventory, BOM records, and attachments you create in the app.

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 16 日

Last updated: September 16, 2026

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于“订单台账”移动应用。应用用于记录和管理业务订单、采购、库存、BOM 和利润信息。

2. 本地数据存储

订单、采购、库存、BOM 和用户主动添加的附件资料保存在您的设备本地。我们不会自动将这些资料传输给开发者。

应用不提供账户注册、登录或云端同步服务。您可以在设备上编辑或删除本地记录。

3. 设备权限

相机、相册和文件权限仅在您主动拍摄、选择凭证或导入资料时使用。应用不会在您未主动操作时访问这些内容。

4. 导出与分享

当您主动导出 CSV 或使用系统分享功能时，相关文件会交由您选择的系统功能或第三方应用处理。请您根据自身需要决定接收方和共享范围。

5. 广告、共享和儿童

应用不展示广告，也不会出售或共享您的数据。应用面向业务管理场景设计，不面向儿童。

6. 联系我们

如对本政策有疑问，请通过 contact@archie-lab.com 联系我们。

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Order Ledger mobile app. The app helps users record and manage business orders, purchases, inventory, BOM records, and profit information.

2. Local data storage

Orders, purchases, inventory, BOM records, and attachments you add are stored locally on your device. We do not automatically transmit this information to the developer.

The app does not provide account registration, sign-in, or cloud synchronization. You can edit or delete local records on your device.

3. Device permissions

Camera, photo-library, and file permissions are used only when you choose to take a photo, select a document, or import information. The app does not access this content without your action.

4. Exporting and sharing

When you choose to export CSV files or use system sharing, the relevant files are handled by the system feature or third-party app you select. You decide the recipient and scope of each share.

5. Advertising, sharing, and children

The app does not display advertising and does not sell or share your data. It is designed for business management and is not directed to children.

6. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at contact@archie-lab.com.

This policy does not limit rights you may have under applicable law. If the app's functions or data practices materially change, we will update this page and its date.
